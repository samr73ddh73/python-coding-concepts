Pattern

Two-Pointer Technique

Use two pointers traversing from different directions or at different speeds for in-place modifications with O(1) space.

Time: O(n) | Space: O(1)
Important

Always Handle Edge Cases

Don't forget: Check for empty arrays, single elements, and boundary conditions in your solution.

Trick

Modify Right-to-Left

When deleting elements in-place, modify from right to left to avoid index shifting issues.
